Overlapping abrasive flaps wear away gradually and keep exposing fresh grain, so a flap disc cuts consistently through its life and leaves a smoother surface than a bonded grinding wheel. Choose Type 27 for flat finishing work and Type 29 for aggressive stock removal on edges and welds.
